# Deskohub language

This glossary records business terms used by Deskohub products.

## Workspace referrals

**Referrer**:
A Workspace customer who invites another person to become a Workspace customer.
_Avoid_: Affiliate, inviter

**Invited customer**:
A Workspace customer attributed to one referrer through a referral.
_Avoid_: Referee, referred account

**Referrer discount**:
A reservation discount determined by the number of distinct invited customers with a qualifying recent visit. Each eligible person contributes a compounding reduction.
_Avoid_: Referral credit, referral voucher
