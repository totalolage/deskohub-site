# Boardgame Bar opening hours

The site publishes a regular weekly schedule and a limited list of upcoming exceptions in Prague local time.

A dedicated business calendar owns exceptions:

- a closure may cover one or more complete calendar dates;
- replacement hours apply to the date and times declared by a marked timed event; and
- unmarked calendar entries remain private planning information and are never published.

If several valid entries affect the same date, closure takes precedence. Conflicting replacement-hours entries do not produce an arbitrary public answer.

Cancelled, malformed, or unmarked entries are ignored. If exceptions are temporarily unavailable, the site continues to show the regular schedule rather than hiding all opening-hours information.

Passing time alone must not leave yesterday's exceptions visible. The public schedule rolls over on each Prague calendar day and reflects business-calendar changes promptly.
