# Boardgame Bar pricing

~Through September 30, 2026, board game players pay a CZK 50 entrance fee. Children under 15 and customers visiting on Mondays do not pay the fee.~

~From October 1, 2026, customers do not pay an entrance fee. A single CZK 250 consumption credit per person applies every day of the week.~

Board game players pay a CZK 50 entrance fee, or CZK 100 if they do not order anything. Children under 15 and customers visiting on Mondays do not pay the fee.

From January 1, 2027, entrace fees will be based on consumption as follows:

- No consumption: CZK 150
- < CZK 200 consumption: CZK 90
- < CZK 400 consumption: CZK 50
- \>= CZK 400 consumption: free
