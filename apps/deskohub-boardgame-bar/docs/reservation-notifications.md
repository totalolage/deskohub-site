# Boardgame Bar reservation notifications

For a training-room request, the reservations team receives the submitted contact and booking details together with a clear instruction to call the customer for confirmation. This notification is required before the request is accepted.

The customer receives a localized acknowledgement containing the requested date, time, and duration and explaining that the team will confirm the request by phone. This acknowledgement does not itself confirm availability or create a binding reservation.

Reservation messages use the published Deskohub sender identity and reservations contact. Customer replies go to the business workflow responsible for the request.

Contact and reservation details are used only for delivering and handling the request. They must not be exposed in public diagnostics or unrelated customer communications.
