# Boardgame Bar reservations

## Table reservations

Customers reserve bar tables through the dedicated booking experience embedded in the Boardgame Bar site. The booking service owns availability, reservation state, and subsequent reservation management. Deskohub does not create a second local table-reservation record.

The booking experience follows the selected site language. If table reservations are not currently offered, their page and entry points are unavailable.

## Training-room requests

A training-room form collects the customer's identity, contact details, requested date, time, duration, and optional special requirements. Submitting the form is a request, not a confirmed reservation.

Deskohub accepts the request only after it has notified the reservations team. The customer is then shown that the request was received and that the team will call during business hours to confirm availability and details.

If the customer acknowledgement cannot be delivered after the reservations team has received the request, the request remains accepted. The customer may contact the published reservations address for follow-up.

If training-room reservations are not currently offered, their pages and entry points are unavailable.
